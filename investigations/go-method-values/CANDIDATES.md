[the historical record](../go-named-results/CANDIDATES.md). Subsequent work:

| Candidate | Measured result | Decision |

Five public projects were screened for typed selector values: Gin, go-redis,
bbolt, GORM, LevelDB. Raw selectors include fields, so raw record counts are not
called missing methods. A local method-definition screen found suitable go-redis
callbacks; the full graph then isolated actual cross-file production references.
Same-file bbolt callbacks were not promoted as focused-context impact. Method
expressions seen in LevelDB are a different unimplemented syntax, not bundled.

The full issue snapshot contains 485 non-PR issues, all open/closed states through
#596, retrieved 7 November 2026. The mechanism-specific audit records every body
hash, query terms and nearest-report comment results. No issue is filed by this
work. Originality is an evidence-backed search conclusion, not a guarantee about
unpublished research or future filings.

Major strong competing reports remain #360 (receiver targets), #364 (generic
calls), #395 (producer/consumer transition links), #409 (published ceilings),
#436 (source identity), #455 (inherited aliases), and #596 (named results).
Several have naturally captured hosted replies and runtime oracles. Our strongest
advantages here are a narrow fresh cause, explicit reference/invocation semantics,
compiling controls, both engines, actual context exports, exhaustive target
coverage, restoration, and independent compiler cases. No official ranking or
first-place claim follows. Additional repetition of duplicated findings would
not improve eligibility.
